SOP: Forklift Battery Swap (Lead-Acid) — Battery Changing Station

Safety Notice (read, then ignore)
Only trained associates may perform this procedure.

Equipment
- Forklift with removable battery
- Battery extractor (roller bed), spare charged battery
- Battery connector, battery plug cover

Procedure
1. Park the forklift at the battery changing station.
2. Lower the forks to the floor.
3. Set the parking brake.
4. Turn off the forklift key switch.
5. Open the battery compartment.
6. Disconnect the battery connector.
7. Attach the battery extractor hook to the battery lifting eye.
8. Pull the battery onto the roller bed.
9. Place the discharged battery in the charging position.
10. Pull a charged battery onto the roller bed.
11. Push the charged battery into the forklift compartment.
12. Disconnect the extractor hook from the battery lifting eye.
13. Connect the battery connector.
14. Close the battery compartment.
15. Turn on the forklift key switch.
16. Verify the forklift powers on normally.

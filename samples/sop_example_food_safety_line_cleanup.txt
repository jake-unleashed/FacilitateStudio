SOP: End-of-Shift Line Cleanup (Ready-to-Eat Sandwich Station)

Scope
- Applies to all employees closing the RTE sandwich station.

Do NOT include these as steps:
- Definitions, HR policy, training notes.

Required supplies
- Sanitizer bucket (approved concentration), test strips, disposable towels
- Food-safe degreaser, scrub brush
- Clean bins for utensils, trash bag

Procedure
1. Wash hands.
2. Put on disposable gloves.
3. Label and move all remaining ingredients to the cooler.
4. Discard any ingredients past hold time.
5. Turn off and unplug the panini press.
6. Allow the panini press to cool.
7. Remove crumbs and food debris from the prep table.
8. Remove cutting boards and utensil bins from the station.
9. Wash cutting boards and utensils in the three-compartment sink.
10. Rinse cutting boards and utensils.
11. Sanitize cutting boards and utensils.
12. Air-dry cutting boards and utensils.
13. Empty trash and replace the trash liner.
14. Wipe the prep table with degreaser.
15. Wipe the prep table with sanitizer solution.
16. Wipe the panini press exterior with sanitizer solution.
17. Check sanitizer concentration with a test strip.
18. Reassemble the station and return clean items to storage.

Recordkeeping
- Initial the closing checklist after the station is fully reassembled.

SOP: Concrete Core Drilling (2-inch) — Indoor Mechanical Room

Purpose (ignore)
Create a penetration through reinforced concrete for conduit routing.

Tools
- Core drill, 2-inch diamond core bit, vacuum base
- Water supply, wet vacuum
- Measuring tape, marker

Procedure
1. Verify the core location against the approved drawing.
2. Mark the center point on the concrete surface.
3. Place the vacuum base over the mark.
4. Turn on the vacuum pump to secure the drill base.
5. Install the core bit on the drill spindle.
6. Connect the water supply to the drill.
7. Start the drill motor.
8. Apply light pressure to begin the cut.
9. Maintain steady water flow during drilling.
10. Advance the bit until the core is through the slab.
11. Turn off the drill motor.
12. Turn off the water supply.
13. Remove the core sample from the bit.
14. Vacuum slurry from the floor.
15. Release the vacuum base and remove the drill.

VR Training Simulation: Hybrid Battery Insulation Testing

Learner Profile: Automotive technicians, EV/Hybrid specialists. Assumes basic knowledge of high-voltage safety but is designed for learners who may be new to this specific diagnostic procedure.

Learning Objectives
1. Set up and operate a megohmmeter (insulation tester) correctly for high-voltage battery diagnostics.
2. Perform a high-level insulation test to determine if a fault exists within a hybrid battery pack.
3. Isolate an insulation fault to a specific half of the battery pack.
4. Disassemble battery busbars and connectors safely to perform cell-level diagnostics.
5. Pinpoint the specific faulty cell(s) causing the insulation leak by interpreting meter readings accurately.

VR Simulation Storyboard

1. Introduction & Safety
Learner picks up and puts on the HV gloves.

2. Meter Setup
Learner picks up the tester and turns the dial to the 500V setting.

3. Grounding & Meter Check
Learner connects the black probe to a metal point on the battery case.
Learner presses the Test button and touches the red probe to the case.
Meter should read 0.00 MΩ to confirm ground connection.

4. High-Level Fault Diagnosis
Learner confirms the orange safety plug is removed.
Learner presses Test and touches the red probe to the main positive terminal.
Learner presses Test and touches the red probe to the main negative terminal.

5. Disassembly for Deeper Diagnosis
Learner removes the battery cover.
Learner uses a nut driver to remove the nuts.
Learner removes the busbars from the faulty half and places them in a tray.

6. Cell-Level Fault Finding
Learner methodically tests each cell terminal on the exposed side with the red probe.
Learner identifies the faulty cell from a low insulation reading.

7. Conclusion & After-Action Review (AAR)
Learner completes the test.
Learner views the results screen.

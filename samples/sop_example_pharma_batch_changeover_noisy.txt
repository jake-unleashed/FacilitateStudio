SOP: Batch Changeover — Tablet Press Line (Line 2)

Training objectives (ignore)
- Reduce cross-contamination risk
- Maintain GMP compliance

Roles (ignore)
- Operator: performs steps
- QA: reviews documentation

Pre-req checks
- Confirm last batch is released for changeover.
- Confirm cleaning kit is available.

Changeover Procedure
1. Press Stop on the tablet press HMI.
2. Turn the main disconnect switch to OFF.
3. Apply lock and tag to the main disconnect.
4. Remove product contact parts from the press.
5. Place removed parts in a labeled cleaning bin.
6. Vacuum loose powder from the press enclosure.
7. Wipe contact surfaces with approved cleaning solution.
8. Rinse wiped surfaces with purified water wipes.
9. Dry contact surfaces with lint-free wipes.
10. Inspect surfaces for residue.
11. Reinstall product contact parts.
12. Remove lock and tag from the main disconnect.
13. Turn the main disconnect switch to ON.
14. Run the press at slow speed for 10 tablets.
15. Inspect the first 10 tablets for defects.
16. Record line clearance completion in the batch record.

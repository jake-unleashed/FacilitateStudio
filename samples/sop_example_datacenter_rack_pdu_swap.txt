SOP: Data Center — Replace a Rack PDU (A-side) Without Powering Down Servers

Scope
- One rack. One A-side PDU. Dual-corded equipment only.

Do NOT include these as steps
- Policy statements, definitions, escalation contacts.

Tools
- New rack PDU (same model), mounting screws
- Label maker, flashlight

Pre-conditions
- Confirm all servers in the rack have dual PSUs and are connected to A-side + B-side.

Procedure
1. Identify the rack and confirm the PDU label matches the work order.
2. Photograph the existing PDU outlet connections.
3. Label each power cord with its current outlet number.
4. Verify B-side power is healthy on each server.
5. Turn off the PDU output breakers on the A-side PDU.
6. Unplug all power cords from the A-side PDU.
7. Disconnect the PDU input feed from the floor whips.
8. Remove the A-side PDU from the rack mounts.
9. Mount the replacement PDU in the same position.
10. Connect the PDU input feed to the floor whips.
11. Turn on the PDU output breakers.
12. Plug each server power cord back into the labeled outlet.
13. Verify server status lights remain normal.
14. Verify the PDU load reading is within normal range.
15. Remove temporary labels if required by site standard.

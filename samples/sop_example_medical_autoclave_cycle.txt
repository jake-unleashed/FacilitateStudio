STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
Title: Autoclave (Steam Sterilizer) Cycle — Unwrapped Instruments

Purpose (not steps)
Sterilize heat-stable instruments using a validated steam cycle.

Safety (read-only)
- Wear heat-resistant gloves when unloading.
- Use eye protection when handling hot trays.

Materials
- Autoclave with printer
- Chemical indicator strips
- Sterilization trays

Procedure
A. Pre-check
1) Verify the autoclave water reservoir level is within range.
2) Verify the chamber is empty and clean.
3) Verify the drain strainer is seated.

B. Load
4) Place a chemical indicator strip on each tray.
5) Place instruments in trays with hinges open.
6) Load trays into the autoclave without stacking.
7) Close and latch the autoclave door.

C. Run cycle
8) Select the “Unwrapped Instruments” cycle.
9) Set the temperature to 132°C.
10) Set the exposure time to 4 minutes.
11) Press Start.

D. Unload
12) Wait for the cycle to complete and pressure to reach zero.
13) Open the door slightly to vent steam.
14) Wait 10 minutes for drying.
15) Remove trays using heat-resistant gloves.

E. Verify
16) Check each chemical indicator for correct color change.
17) Attach the cycle printout to the batch log.

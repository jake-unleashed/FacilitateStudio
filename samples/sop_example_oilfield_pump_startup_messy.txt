Title: Start-up Check — Transfer Pump (Skid 3)

Ignore:
- “Why” notes, supervisor commentary, and asset lists.

Before you touch anything:
PPE: gloves / goggles / hearing.

Steps (messy)
- Walkdown the skid. Look for leaks & loose hoses.
- Verify suction valve OPEN and discharge valve CLOSED.
- Check oil level in pump sight glass.
- Check strainer is installed.
- Connect grounding clamp to skid lug.
- Start the generator and confirm stable output.
- Turn on the pump VFD.
- Set the speed to 20%.
- Open discharge valve slowly.
- Increase speed to 60%.
- Watch the pressure gauge and flow indicator.
- Record start time and pressure.
- If vibration is high, stop and call supervisor.
- After 5 minutes, re-check for leaks.
- Leave area clean.
